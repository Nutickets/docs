## Overview

A payment is either processed by the platform's gateway, or taken on your own merchant account and recorded here afterwards.

The platform can only return money it moved. A gateway-processed payment leaves a gateway-backed transaction on the order, which online refunds, [cash outs](/user-manual/account-balances/cash-outs) to the original card, [resale](/user-manual/resale/introduction) payouts and [order modifications](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications) all rely on. A payment recorded after the fact leaves no gateway reference, so those operations are unavailable on that order.

This is a separate decision from the [integration model](/integrate/integration-models). Our checkout, an embed and a basket link all process payment on the platform; an API-completed order can go either way.

## The Two Routes

| | Platform-processed | Off-platform |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant account | The company's, connected to the platform | Your own |
| How you get there | Our checkout, or the [Payments API](#option-2-your-ui-our-gateway) | Take payment yourself, then record it |
| What lands on the order | A gateway-backed transaction | A record that payment happened |
| Online refunds | Yes | No |
| Refund to account balance | Yes | Yes, where the feature is enabled and the order has a customer |
| Cash outs and resale payouts to the original card | Yes | No |

Three ways to get there. Our checkout and the Payments API are both platform-processed; taking the money yourself is not.

## Option 1: Our Checkout Takes the Payment

The customer pays in our checkout, reached by [link](/integrate/checkout/linking-out), inside an [embedded frame](/integrate/checkout/embedding), or through a [basket link](/user-manual/technical/basket-links) generated from an API-built basket.

This is the recommended route, including for integrations that build their own basket — [handing the basket back](/integrate/checkout/building-baskets#handing-the-basket-back) keeps your buying UI and leaves payment with us.

It covers:

- **Every gateway the platform supports** — around 25, changed in settings rather than in your integration. See [Payment Processors](/user-manual/payments/payment-processors).
- **Methods layered on the gateway** — Apple Pay and Google Pay, saved cards, Stripe Link, and local or buy-now-pay-later methods such as Klarna, iDEAL, Bancontact and OXXO where the gateway offers them.
- **Non-gateway methods** — [account balance](/user-manual/account-balances/top-ups) spending, gift vouchers, [split payments](/user-manual/payments/split-payments), [deposits](/user-manual/payments/deposit-payments) and [invoice payments](/user-manual/payments/invoice-payments).
- **3D Secure, SCA and gateway failure handling**, including gateways that cannot render inside a frame and have to open at top level. See [Leaving the Frame](/integrate/reference/url-parameters#leaving-the-frame).
- **Post-payment operations** — online refunds, [refund protection](/user-manual/refund-protection/protect-group), [resale](/user-manual/resale/introduction) payouts and [order modifications](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications).

Confirm the sale server-side rather than from the browser. Set `successRedirectUrl` and `failureRedirectUrl` to return the customer to you, then verify with the [Order completed webhook](/user-manual/technical/webhooks) or [Retrieve Order](/api-reference/orders/retrieve-order). See [Bringing Them Back Afterwards](/integrate/checkout/building-baskets#bringing-them-back-afterwards).

## Option 2: Your UI, Our Gateway

The Payments API renders the payment step in your own interface while still processing through the company's connected gateway, so the transaction is gateway-backed and refunds behave normally.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the basket">
    [Add To Basket](/api-reference/baskets/add-to-basket), without `autocomplete`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a payment intent">
    [Create Payment Intent](/api-reference/payments/create-payment-intent) prepares the payment against the basket and returns what you need to confirm it client-side, along with a `transactionId`. Set `cardPresent` for in-person payments, or `interacPresent` for Interac in Canada.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the payment">
    In the customer's browser, or on your terminal, using the payment provider's own client SDK. Card-present flows also need a [connection token](/api-reference/payments/retrieve-connection-token) for the reader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the order">
    [Complete Order](/api-reference/orders/complete-order) with the `basketId`, the `transactionId` from the intent, and `capture: true`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The Payments API supports Stripe only, and rejects any other gateway with a validation error. On any other gateway the options are our checkout or Option 3. See [Payment Processors](/user-manual/payments/payment-processors).
</Warning>

Three things to account for:

- **`capture` defaults to `false`** for backwards compatibility, so omitting it completes the order against an uncaptured payment. Set it explicitly. If capture cannot be confirmed the call returns a `422`, the order stays incomplete, and the transaction stays open for retry.
- **The integration user needs the box office selling permission.** Creating a payment intent requires **Mobile box office: sell items**; a token scoped only to reading events and writing baskets is rejected with a `403`. See [Permissions Reference](/user-manual/system-access/permissions-reference#mobile-apps).
- **This route collects nothing the checkout collects.** [Data capture](/user-manual/data-capture/introduction), terms, [refund protection](/user-manual/refund-protection/protect-group) and upsells are yours to build or omit.

<Note>
Deposits are supported — pass `paymentPlan` with type `deposit` on the intent and on completion. See [Deposit Payments](/user-manual/payments/deposit-payments).
</Note>

## Option 3: You Take the Payment

Payment runs on your own merchant account, and the order is completed afterwards with the method that describes it:

| Method | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| `card-not-present-offline` | A card payment taken on your own online checkout |
| `card-present-offline` | A card payment taken on your own terminal |
| `cash`, `bank`, `cheque`, `money-order` | Payment taken outside the platform in that form |
| `complimentary` | No payment |

If you have already taken the money you are recording it rather than processing it, which is what the `-offline` methods are for. [Completing Orders with the API](/integrate/checkout/api-checkout) covers the mechanics.

Use the `-offline` variants rather than `card-not-present-online` or `card-present-online`. The `-online` methods describe a payment the platform's gateway processed. Recording an off-platform payment as one produces an order that appears refundable online; the refund attempt then falls back to the company's default gateway and fails there rather than at validation.

## What Off-Platform Payment Changes

An **online refund** — *"An online refund will cancel the items in this system as well as attempt to return the money to the customer via their original payment method"* — is offered only where the original transaction went through a payment gateway. Whether that gateway was reached through our checkout or the Payments API makes no difference.

On an order recorded offline, the refund screen shows **No items can be refunded online**, and *"Item cannot be refunded online."* per item. What remains:

| Refund type | On an off-platform order |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| **Refund online** | Unavailable |
| **Refund offline** | Available. *"An offline refund will cancel the items in this system but no money will be returned to the customer."* You return the money in your own system |
| **Refund to balance** | Available where **Account Balance Refunds** is enabled for the company and the order has a customer. Credits the customer's [account balance](/user-manual/account-balances/top-ups) instead of their card |
| **Cancel items** | Available. Cancels without a monetary refund |

Through the API, [Refund Order](/api-reference/orders/refund-order) and [Refund Order Items](/api-reference/orders/refund-order-items) accept `online: true`, but on a transaction with no payment gateway behind it the flag has no effect and an offline refund is recorded. The call still returns `200`, so check `refundStatus` rather than the status code.

Two other operations depend on a gateway-backed payment:

- **[Cash outs](/user-manual/account-balances/cash-outs) to the original payment method.** Otherwise only recordable offline.
- **[Resale](/user-manual/resale/introduction) payouts to the original payment method.** Sellers are paid to their account balance instead.

[Order modifications](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications) still work, but a later refund of the replacement order returns only the part paid through the gateway.

<Note>
A gateway reference cannot be attached to an order after the fact, so this applies for the life of every order taken this way.
</Note>

## Which to Choose

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="You want to sell tickets">
  Option 1. Link, embed, or build the basket through the API and hand it back with a basket link. All three process payment on the platform, and the basket link keeps your own buying UI.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="You need the payment step in your own interface">
  Option 2, if the company is on Stripe. On any other gateway, weigh a branded checkout — [custom URL](/user-manual/utilities/custom-urls), [theme](/user-manual/cms/theme-and-branding), [menu](/user-manual/cms/menus) — against losing online refunds.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="You have to use your own merchant account">
  Option 3. Record with an `-offline` method and handle refunds in your own system. Check first whether **Refund to balance** covers enough of your cases to use instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Nobody is paying at the point of sale">
  The question does not arise. Comps, entitlements, allocations, migrated orders and renewals charged elsewhere complete with `complimentary` or an `-offline` method. See [Completing Orders with the API](/integrate/checkout/api-checkout).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="You are mixing routes">
  Common — an embedded shop for the public, API-completed orders for a partner feed. Keep the payment methods accurate on each; filter [Retrieve Transactions](/api-reference/transactions/retrieve-transactions) by `type` to separate gateway payments from recorded ones.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
