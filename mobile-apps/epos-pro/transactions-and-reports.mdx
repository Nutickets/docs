## Overview

<Tip>Redesigned in Release 48</Tip>

Three home screen tiles cover what happens after the sale: **Transactions** for the order history and refunds, **Sales** for the shop's running totals by payment method, and **Catalog** for product prices and stock.

## Transaction History

Tap **Transactions** on the home screen to view EPOS sales and top-ups, most recent first. This tile requires the **View list of Orders** permission.

The list refreshes when you open it and in the background while the app is running — pull down to refresh it immediately. Each transaction shows its order reference, total, customer name and email, and a status line with the date and time:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Completed at:** | The order is complete with nothing refunded |
| **Partially refunded at:** | Some of the order's items have been refunded |
| **Refunded at:** | Every refundable item has been refunded — the total is shown struck through |

### Searching

The **Search transactions** bar filters the list by:

- Order reference (partial match)
- Customer name (partial match)
- Customer ID (exact match)

In NFC mode, tapping a customer's NFC tag on this screen fills the search with their customer ID, showing only that customer's transactions.

### Transaction Details

Tap a transaction to open it. The latest details are loaded from the platform:

- **Order summary** — the order reference, total, customer name and email, status, and **Payment method**
- **Order Items** — each item with its price

If a printer is available, **Print Receipt** prints a [receipt](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/settings-and-hardware#receipt-printing) for the order (**Print receipt again** once one has been printed).

<Note>
If the details can't be loaded, the app shows *"Loading transaction was unsuccessful. Please make sure you have internet connection and try again."* with a **Try again** button.
</Note>

## Refunds

Refunds are processed from the transaction details screen. You can refund individual items without affecting the rest of the order. Refunding requires the **Mobile box office: issue refund** permission — without it, the refund button doesn't appear.

### Processing a Refund

1. Open the transaction you want to refund
2. Tick the checkbox next to each item to refund
3. Tap **Refund 1 item** or **Refund [number] items**
4. Confirm the refund (see below)

Items that have already been refunded, and top-up items, have no checkbox. Top-up credit is returned to a customer through a [cash-out](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/topups-and-cashout#cash-out) instead.

### Confirming the Refund

How the refund is confirmed depends on how the order was paid:

- **Paid by account balance, in NFC mode** — the app shows *"Tap NFC tag to confirm refund"*. The customer taps their tag, which must belong to the customer who made the purchase. Otherwise the refund is blocked with *"Refund is not possible, as this NFC tag doesn't belong to [name] who made this transaction."*
- **All other orders** — the app asks for confirmation: *"An online refund will cancel the items in this system as well as attempt to return the money to the customer via their original payment method."* Tap **Confirm**.

### Where the Money Goes

| Paid by | Refund |
|---------|--------|
| **Account balance** | The amount is returned to the customer's [account balance](/user-manual/account-balances/top-ups) |
| **Card** or **Tap to Pay** | The amount is refunded to the customer's card |
| **Cash** or **Card (offline)** | The items are refunded on the platform, but no money is moved — return it to the customer yourself |

Once the refund succeeds, the details reload with the refunded items removed from selection, and the order's status changes to **Partially refunded at:** or **Refunded at:**. If it fails, the app shows the reason given by the platform, or *"Transaction failed"*.

## Sales

Tap **Sales** on the home screen to see the shop's sales totals. This tile requires the **Mobile box office: view reports** permission.

The figures are all-time totals for the selected shop, loaded from the platform — they include sales from every device selling for the shop, not just this one. Pull down to refresh them.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total NET** | All sales, including account balance sales, minus refunds |
| **Cash sales** | Revenue from cash payments |
| **Card sales** | Revenue from card payments |
| **eWallet online sales** | Revenue from [account balance](/user-manual/account-balances/top-ups) payments |
| **eWallet offline sales** | Revenue from offline account balance payments |
| **Online refunds** | Refunds returned through a payment method or account balance |
| **Offline refunds** | Refunds made offline, such as cash refunds |

<Note>
If the totals can't be loaded, the screen shows *"Please make sure you have Internet Connection"* with a **Refresh** button, or *"No report exists"* if the platform returns no report. For sales by shop or by staff member, use the [EPOS reports](/user-manual/reporting/default-reports#epos-reports) in the admin panel.
</Note>

## Catalog

Tap **Catalog** on the home screen to check prices and stock. The catalogue lists every product in the shop — including products hidden from **Select products** because they're off sale or out of stock — and refreshes each time you open it (pull down to refresh again). Use **Search products** to filter by name.

Each product shows:

- Its name, product ID (for example **#123**), category, and price
- **Sold** — how many have been sold
- **Remaining** — how many are left, for products with limited stock
- **Total** — the total stock, or **Unlimited**
- A bar showing how much of the stock has sold
