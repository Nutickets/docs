## Overview

<Tip>Redesigned in Release 48</Tip>

**Top-ups** add credit to a customer's [account balance](/user-manual/account-balances/top-ups), which they can then spend at your EPOS shops. **Cash-outs** pay their remaining balance back to them in cash. Both start from the home screen and identify the customer by NFC tag or QR code. Nothing is charged or paid out until the top-up or cash-out is confirmed — by a second tap of the customer's NFC tag, or a confirmation prompt in QR mode.

## Top-Ups

Tap **Top up** on the home screen to begin. This tile is visible when you have the **Mobile Epos: Issue Top-up** permission.

### Choosing the Amount

How the amount is chosen depends on the EPOS currency:

- **Standard currency (e.g. GBP, USD)** — the **Enter topup amount** sheet opens as soon as the screen does. Type the amount to add to the customer's balance and confirm with the keyboard's done key.
- **[Custom currency](/user-manual/epos/custom-currencies) (e.g. Tokens)** — the screen lists your [top-up items](/user-manual/account-balances/top-ups#creating-a-top-up) that grant the custom currency, each showing its price and the credit it adds (for example *"£10.00 → 10 Tokens"*). Tap one to select it. Tablets show the list in two columns.

The chosen amount appears in the **Total cost** bar at the bottom of the screen. If the platform adds a [fee](/user-manual/account-balances/top-ups#fees) to the top-up, it's shown underneath as **+ [amount] fee**, and the payment total includes it. Tap the **Total cost** bar to change the amount, or — with a custom currency — to clear your selection and pick a different item.

<Note>
Top-up items that don't grant any custom currency, or whose price is in a currency that isn't available, aren't listed. If none of your items can be sold, the screen shows *"Top-up prices are unavailable. Please try again shortly."*
</Note>

### Identifying the Customer

- **NFC mode** — the screen shows *"Tap NFC tag to assign customer"*. The customer taps their tag against the device.
- **QR mode** — the camera opens (*"Scan ticket's QR code"*). Scan the QR code on the customer's ticket, or use **Manual Entry** to type it.

Once identified, the screen shows the customer's name, their **Current balance**, and their **Balance after topup**.

### Taking Payment

The payment buttons appear below the **Total cost** bar and become available once both an amount and a customer are set. Top-ups are paid in real money, so the options are the card and cash methods from the [basket](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/selling-products#payment-methods) — **Card**, **Tap to Pay**, **Card (offline)**, and **Cash** — following the same [payment settings](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/settings-and-hardware#payment-settings). Account balance and complimentary payments aren't offered for top-ups.

With **Cash input and change** turned on, **Cash** first opens a sheet titled **Total cost** where you enter the cash received; the sheet shows the change due, and *"Cash received must cover the topup amount"* if the amount is too low.

### Confirming the Top-Up

Before any payment is taken, the customer confirms the top-up:

- **NFC mode** — the app shows *"Tap NFC tag to confirm top up"* and the customer taps their tag again. A different customer's tag stops the top-up with *"Top up is not possible, as this NFC tag doesn't belong to [name]."*
- **QR mode** — the app asks *"Top up [amount] for [name]?"* (or, with a custom currency, *"Take [price] and credit [credits] to [name]?"*) — tap to confirm.

Card and Tap to Pay top-ups then continue on the [card payment screen](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/selling-products#card-payments-with-tap-to-pay-or-a-stripe-reader). Once the payment succeeds, the **Order complete!** screen shows the payment details and the customer's new balance, and a [receipt](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/settings-and-hardware#receipt-printing) can be printed, including the balance.

<Note>
Top-ups can't be refunded from the [Transactions](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/transactions-and-reports#refunds) screen. To return unspent top-up credit to a customer, use a [cash-out](#cash-out).
</Note>

## Cash Out

Tap **Cash out** on the home screen to begin. This tile is visible when you have the **Mobile Epos: Cash-out** permission.

<Note>
Cash-outs aren't available when EPOS uses a [custom currency](/user-manual/epos/custom-currencies). Tapping **Cash out** shows *"Cash out is not possible with custom currencies"*.
</Note>

### Identifying the Customer

Tap the customer's NFC tag (*"Tap NFC tag to assign customer"*) or scan the QR code on their ticket, depending on the authentication mode. The screen shows the customer's name, **Current balance**, and **Balance after cash out**.

### Choosing the Amount

<Tip>Partial cash-outs added in Release 48</Tip>

The **Cash out amount** bar is pre-filled with the most the customer can cash out — their whole balance, or less if part of it isn't eligible. Only unspent top-ups count towards what can be returned; see [Refundable Balance](/user-manual/account-balances/cash-outs#refundable-balance). When the maximum is below the customer's balance, the screen shows *"Maximum available to cash out: [amount]"*.

To pay out less than the maximum, tap the **Cash out amount** bar and enter a smaller amount in **Enter cash out amount**. Amounts above the maximum are rejected with *"Cash out cannot exceed [amount]"*.

If there's nothing to return, the app shows one of:

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| *"There is nothing to cash out from the account belonging to [name]."* | The customer's balance is empty. |
| *"None of the balance belonging to [name] is available to cash out."* | The customer has a balance, but none of it came from unspent top-ups. |

### Confirming the Cash-Out

Tap **Cash out**, then confirm:

- **NFC mode** — the app shows *"Tap NFC tag to confirm cash out"* and the customer taps their tag again. A different customer's tag stops the cash-out with *"Cash out is not possible, as this NFC tag doesn't belong to [name]."*
- **QR mode** — the app asks *"Cash out [amount] to [name]?"* — tap to confirm.

The app records the cash-out on the platform as an [offline cash-out](/user-manual/account-balances/cash-outs#online-vs-offline): the amount is taken off the customer's balance, and you hand the equivalent cash to the customer.

### After the Cash-Out

The **Order complete!** screen shows the **Amount cashed out**, the customer's name, and their **New balance**. A receipt prints automatically when **Customer receipt** is turned on in [settings](/mobile-apps/epos-pro/settings-and-hardware#receipt-printing), or tap **Print Receipt**. Tap **Close** to return to the home screen.

<Note>
If the cash-out fails, the app shows the reason given by the platform, or *"Transaction failed"* if the connection dropped. Retrying for the same customer and amount reuses the original request, so a cash-out that already went through isn't recorded twice.
</Note>
