## Overview

Settings control which payment methods are available, how the app interacts with hardware, and optional features like kiosk mode and NFC pairing. Access them from the menu on the event listing screen.

## Payment Settings

Payment methods can be individually enabled or disabled. At least one payment method must remain active at all times — the app prevents you from disabling the last one.

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Cash payments** | On | Accept cash payments. |
| **Card payments** | Off | Accept online card payments (card not present), processed via Stripe payment sheet on the device. |
| **Card (offline) payments** | Off | Record card-present payments processed outside the app. |
| **Terminal card payments** | Off | Accept card payments via a connected Stripe reader. Enabling this opens the Stripe reader settings if no reader is connected. |
| **Split payments** | Off | Allow staff to combine multiple payment methods for a single order. Only visible if split payments are enabled for your account. |
| **Interac support** | Off | Accept Interac debit payments (Canada only). Only available when terminal card payments are enabled. |

<Note>
**Card payments** and **Terminal card payments** cannot both be enabled at the same time. Enabling one automatically disables the other.
</Note>

### Cash Input and Change

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Cash input and change** | On | When enabled, a numeric input prompts staff to enter the cash received. The app calculates and displays the change. When disabled, cash payments complete immediately. Only available when cash payments are enabled. |

## Stripe Card Reader

Box Office Pro integrates with [Stripe](/user-manual/payments/payment-processors) Terminal for in-person card payments. Three reader types are supported:

| Reader Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Bluetooth** | An external Stripe reader connected via Bluetooth. |
| **USB** | An external Stripe reader connected via a USB cable to the device. Useful for stationary point-of-sale setups where wired connectivity is more reliable than Bluetooth. |
| **Tap to pay** | Uses the device's built-in NFC for contactless card acceptance. No external reader required. Available on any device that Stripe supports for Tap to Pay. |

On devices that support multiple methods, you can combine Bluetooth or USB with Tap to Pay to allow either type to be used interchangeably.

<Tip>Android 13 requirement for Tap to pay removed in Release 48</Tip>

The app doesn't apply its own minimum Android version to Tap to pay — the Tap to pay connection options appear in the reader menu whenever Stripe confirms the device supports it, so devices running Android versions earlier than 13 can use Tap to pay provided Stripe supports them. NFC must be switched on in the device settings to take a Tap to pay payment.

### Connecting a Reader

The Stripe reader controls are reached from the toolbar menu on the event listing screen. Open the menu and choose one of:

| Option | Connects |
|--------|----------|
| **Connect: Bluetooth** | A single Bluetooth reader |
| **Connect: USB** | A single USB reader |
| **Connect: Tap to pay** | The device's built-in NFC reader |
| **Connect: BT + TTP** | A Bluetooth reader plus the device's Tap to pay |
| **Connect: USB + TTP** | A USB reader plus the device's Tap to pay |
| **Disconnect** | Removes the current reader (asks for confirmation: *"Are you sure?"*), or cancels a [reconnection](#automatic-reconnection) that's in progress |

The first time you connect, you'll be prompted to choose a **location** — these are configured in your Stripe Dashboard under **Payments > Readers > Locations**. Once a location is set, the app discovers readers nearby and displays them in a list — tap a reader to connect.

While discovering, the screen shows *"Discovering"*. Once readers are found it switches to *"Discovered readers"* with each reader's serial number. While connecting, the screen shows *"Connecting reader..."*. If the reader requires a software update, the progress is shown on screen and you must keep the device connected until it completes.

<Note>
If no locations are configured in your Stripe Dashboard, the app displays: *"Please select a location"*. Add at least one location in Stripe Dashboard under **Payments > Readers > Locations** before continuing.
</Note>

<Note>
If the app can't start its card reader service, opening the reader controls shows *"Card reader unavailable: the app needs location, Bluetooth and camera permissions, and location services switched on. Check them in Settings, then return to the app."* Grant the permissions and switch on location services in the device settings, then return to the app.
</Note>

Signing out disconnects the reader and clears the saved reader and location, so the next account to sign in on the device starts from its own Stripe locations. If connecting later fails because the saved location no longer exists in the Stripe account, the app shows *"The saved reader location no longer exists in this Stripe account. Choose a location from the reader menu and try again."* — use **Change location** in the reader menu to pick another.

### Automatic Reconnection

When the app starts, it reconnects to the saved Bluetooth or USB reader you last used.

The **Enable automatic reader reconnection** setting (on by default) controls whether a connected reader tries to re-establish a dropped connection on its own. While a reconnection attempt is in progress, the reader menu shows *"Status: Connecting"* — tap **Disconnect** to cancel the attempt if you'd rather connect a different reader. <Tip>Cancelling a reconnection added in version 3.7.3</Tip> Turn the setting off if you prefer to reconnect readers manually after a dropped connection.

#### Tap to Pay Recovery

<Tip>Automatic Tap to Pay recovery added in Release 48</Tip>

When Tap to pay is the connected reader, the app recovers the connection itself if it drops unexpectedly — for example, after a period of inactivity — so staff don't have to reconnect it from the reader menu between sales. Recovery runs whether or not **Enable automatic reader reconnection** is on:

1. Shortly after the disconnect, the app rediscovers and reconnects the device's Tap to pay reader in the background.
2. If that fails, it tries again with progressively longer gaps — up to six attempts over about a minute.
3. If a payment is in progress, recovery waits until the payment has finished.
4. If every attempt fails, the app shows *"Could not reconnect Tap to Pay — reconnect manually from the reader menu"*. Recovery starts again the next time the app comes back to the foreground or you start a card payment, or you can reconnect straight away with **Connect: Tap to pay**.

If the reader's own reconnection attempt fails first, you may briefly see *"Stripe reader disconnected"* before the app's recovery takes over. Tap to pay is also reconnected when the app starts, as long as it was the last reader you connected and you didn't disconnect it yourself.

<Note>
Recovery needs NFC switched on and location permission granted. If NFC is turned off, the app waits and recovers as soon as NFC is turned back on. Recovery doesn't apply to the combined **Connect: BT + TTP** and **Connect: USB + TTP** modes, and it stops if you disconnect Tap to pay yourself or cancel a reconnection — it resumes once you connect Tap to pay again from the reader menu.
</Note>

## Receipt Printer Selection

Box Office Pro can print on two printer brands:

| Printer | Connection | Devices |
|---------|------------|---------|
| **Sunmi** | Built-in thermal printer | Sunmi devices with integrated printer hardware |
| **Masung** | USB | Standalone Masung USB thermal printers attached to a USB-host device |

Open settings and tap **Printer** to switch between Sunmi and Masung. The app uses the selected printer for both ticket and receipt printing — see [Receipt Printing](#receipt-printing) for the relevant printing settings, and [Orders and After the Sale](/mobile-apps/box-office-pro/orders-and-after-the-sale#receipt-printing) for what each printout contains.

<Note>
Masung is detected via USB on connect. If the printer isn't recognised, check that the USB cable is firmly plugged in and that the device has granted USB permission to the app.
</Note>

If the selected printer isn't ready within a few seconds of a print request, the app shows *"Printer not available - nothing was printed"* — check the printer connection and print again.

## Check-In After Purchase

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Check in all the tickets purchased** | Disabled | Controls whether tickets are checked in (admitted) immediately after purchase. Three options: **Disabled**, **Admit button** (manual), or **Auto admit** (automatic). See [Orders and After the Sale](/mobile-apps/box-office-pro/orders-and-after-the-sale#check-in-after-purchase). |

As the option dialog explains, check-in applies to tickets only — including tickets that are part of bundles. Products and season tickets are not admitted, whichever option you choose. See [What Gets Checked In](/mobile-apps/box-office-pro/orders-and-after-the-sale#what-gets-checked-in).

## NFC Pairing

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **NFC pairing** | Off | When enabled, the app enters an NFC pairing flow after each purchase, allowing staff to pair tickets to NFC wristbands or cards. Applies to tickets for events using the **NFC (online)** [scanning mode](/user-manual/event-management/nfc-pairing#setting-the-scanning-mode). See [Orders and After the Sale](/mobile-apps/box-office-pro/orders-and-after-the-sale#nfc-ticket-pairing). |

## Receipt Printing

Receipt printing is available on **Sunmi** devices with a built-in thermal printer, and on connected **Masung** USB thermal printers (see [Receipt Printer Selection](#receipt-printer-selection)).

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Printer paper size** | 80mm | The width of the paper roll in your Sunmi printer: **80mm** or **58mm**. Only shown when **Sunmi** is the selected printer. <Tip>New in Release 48</Tip> |
| **Print receipt** | Off | Automatically prints a financial receipt after printing tickets. When disabled, only tickets are printed. |
| **Cutting paper** | Disabled | Controls paper cutting behaviour. Options: **Disabled**, **After printing order** (single cut after all tickets), or **After each ticket** (cut between each ticket). |

Ticket and receipt layouts are sized to the **Printer paper size** — for example, 80mm receipts print each item's name, barcode, and price side by side, while 58mm receipts move the barcode onto its own line. The app no longer detects the paper width from the printer, so set this to **58mm** on devices with narrower rolls. A change applies from the next printout.

## Strict Ticket Check

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Strict ticket check** | Off | When enabled, the app validates every basket change against the server in real time, checking ticket availability before confirming the addition. When disabled, items are added to a local basket and synced when you navigate to the basket screen. |

## Secure Barcodes

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Disable secure barcodes** | On | When enabled, secure barcode generation is skipped after transactions. This speeds up order completion at the cost of not creating enhanced barcodes. |

## Cash Drawer

On Sunmi devices with a connected cash drawer, the **Cash drawer** button in settings opens the drawer directly. It also appears during cash payments and on the payment completion screen.

Opening the cash drawer requires the appropriate permission assigned to your user role.

## Kiosk Mode

Kiosk mode turns the device into an unattended self-service terminal. Customers can browse events, select tickets, and complete purchases without staff interaction.

### Entering Kiosk Mode

Tap **Enter kiosk mode** in settings. The app switches to a full-screen branded display with a single **Buy Tickets** button. The company logo and brand colours are applied to the kiosk screen.

### Customer Experience

When a customer taps **Buy Tickets**, they are taken through the standard event browsing, ticket selection, and payment flow. After purchase, the app automatically returns to the kiosk screen ready for the next customer.

An inactivity timer monitors for idle periods. If no interaction is detected for 25 seconds, the app resets to the kiosk home screen.

### Exiting Kiosk Mode

Tap the left corner of the screen **three times** to trigger the exit dialog. Enter your admin password and tap **Close kiosk mode**. If the password is incorrect, the app shows *"Wrong password"* and remains in kiosk mode.

<Note>
The menu, settings, and navigation are all hidden while in kiosk mode. Only the exit gesture (triple-tap on the left corner) provides access to the password dialog.
</Note>

## Screen Orientation

The app supports both portrait and landscape modes. A toggle on the login screen (and in settings) switches between orientations. Landscape mode is only available on devices with screens of at least 600dp on the shortest side.

On first launch, the app detects the device's current orientation and sets it as the default.
