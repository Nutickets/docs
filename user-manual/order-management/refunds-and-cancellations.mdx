## Overview

After an order is completed, you may need to return money to a customer (a **refund**) or remove items without returning money (a **cancellation**). Both actions are available from the order detail page in admin, and customers can self-serve refunds to their account balance if you enable that option.

The platform supports several refund scenarios:

- **Online refunds** — Money is returned to the customer's original payment method through the payment gateway
- **Offline refunds** — The refund is recorded in the system but money is returned manually (e.g. cash, bank transfer)
- **Account balance refunds** — The refund amount is credited to the customer's account balance for future purchases
- **Cancellations** — Items are removed from the order without returning any money

## Refunds vs Cancellations

The distinction matters because the financial outcome is different:

| Action | Money returned? | Items removed? | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Refund** | Yes — to original payment method, or to account balance | Yes | Customer wants money back or credit for future use |
| **Cancellation** | No | Yes | Item needs to be removed but no financial return is warranted (e.g. no-show policy, terms violation) |

Both actions restock the cancelled items (releasing capacity back to general sale) and update the order status accordingly.

## Refunding an Order

### Starting a Refund

1. Open the order in admin and click **Refund/cancel items**.
2. Select which items to refund using the checkboxes.
3. Optionally set a [cancellation fee](#cancellation-fees) for the items being refunded.
4. Choose the refund method:
   - **Online** — Refund is processed through the payment gateway back to the customer's original payment method
   - **Offline** — Refund is recorded but you handle the actual money transfer manually
   - **Account balance** — Refund amount is credited to the customer's account balance
5. Optionally add a reason and choose whether to notify the customer by email.
6. Submit the refund.

For orders with more than a few items, the refund form offers a **Bulk** toggle that simplifies the process — see [Cancellation Fees](#cancellation-fees) for how the fee applies in each mode.

### What Happens After a Refund

- **Items are cancelled** — Each refunded item is marked as cancelled and no longer valid
- **Stock is restocked** — Capacity reserved by the cancelled items is released back to general sale
- **Order status updates** — The order moves to **Partially refunded** (if some items remain) or **Refunded** (if all items are refunded)
- **Customer notification** — If enabled, the customer receives an email confirming the refund with details of the amount returned
- **Wallet deductions** — If any refunded items were wallet top-ups, the corresponding balance is deducted from the customer's wallet

<Note>
**Refunds and automatic customer groups.** Refunded and cancelled items no longer count toward an automatic [customer group's](/user-manual/customer-management/customer-groups) purchase criteria. If refunding an item drops a customer below a group's minimum-purchase requirement, they are removed from the group the next time automatic groups re-sync — either on the regular schedule or when you trigger **Re-sync** from the customer or group page.
</Note>

### Online vs Offline Refunds

**Online refunds** are processed automatically through the payment gateway. The money is returned to whichever payment method the customer originally used. This is the most common approach for card payments.

**Offline refunds** are recorded in the system for accounting purposes, but you are responsible for actually returning the money (e.g. handing back cash, making a bank transfer). Use this for orders originally paid by cash, bank transfer, or when the payment gateway doesn't support automated refunds.

<Note>
For orders paid with [split payments](/user-manual/payments/split-payments), the refund is automatically distributed proportionately across the original payment transactions. For example, if a customer paid 60% by card and 40% by wallet, a refund of any item is split 60/40 across those payment methods.
</Note>

### Refunding Cancelled or Partially Refunded Items

<Tip>New in Release 47</Tip>

A cancelled item is no longer a dead end. If an item still has money attached to it, you can issue a refund against it even after it has been cancelled or after part of its value has already been refunded. This supports several common situations:

- **Delayed reimbursement** — An item was cancelled first (for example, to release the seat) with the refund decision deferred until later. You can return the money once the decision is made.
- **Correcting a mistaken cancellation** — An item was cancelled without a refund by mistake, and you now want to give the customer their money back.
- **Refunding fees separately** — You refunded the item value earlier but left the processing or platform fees in place, and now want to return those too.

When you reopen a refund on the order, items that were previously cancelled or refunded remain selectable as long as they still have a refundable balance. Each such item is annotated on the refund page so you know its history:

| Annotation | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Cancelled at [date]** | The item was cancelled. If it still has a refundable balance, it can be refunded. |
| **Refunded on [date]** | The item was previously refunded. The line also shows **Refunded: [amount]** and **Remaining: [amount]**. |
| **Refunded: [amount], Remaining: [amount]** | How much has already been returned and how much is still available to refund. |

The **Remaining** figure is the item total minus whatever has already been refunded — this is the maximum you can return on this pass.

When your selection includes a cancelled item and you are issuing a refund (not a cancellation), the refund page shows a warning so the action is deliberate:

> Your selection includes one or more cancelled tickets. A refund will still be processed for these items.

<Note>
Items whose value was already returned by other means cannot be refunded again. An item that was replaced through a [resale](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications) or an [order modification](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications#refunding-a-modified-order) has had its value moved elsewhere (resale proceeds or a balance transfer to the replacement order), so it stays blocked. Refund the replacement order instead. Items with no remaining balance are also blocked with **Item already refunded**.
</Note>

### Cancellation Fees

When refunding an item, you can apply a **cancellation fee** — a portion of the item price that is retained rather than refunded. The customer receives the item total minus the cancellation fee.

For example, if a ticket costs £50 and you set a £10 cancellation fee, the customer receives a £40 refund.

The maximum cancellation fee for each item is calculated based on the item's available refund amount (total minus any processing or reseller fees that are configured as non-refundable).

#### How the Fee Applies

The cancellation fee behaves differently depending on how you refund:

| Refund mode | How the cancellation fee works | Example (£5 fee, 5 items) |
|---|---|---|
| **Item-by-item** (default) | You set a separate cancellation fee for each item individually. Each item can have a different fee amount. | You could set £5 on one item and £2 on another — total depends on what you enter per item. |
| **Bulk toggle** (single order with many items) | You enter one cancellation fee amount, and that **same fee is applied to every selected item**. | Each of the 5 items is charged £5, so the total cancellation fee is **£25**. |
| **[Bulk refund](#bulk-refunds)** (multiple orders) | You enter one cancellation fee amount, and it is **distributed proportionally** across items in each order based on their refundable amounts. | The £5 is split across the 5 items in proportion to their prices — the total cancellation fee per order is **£5**. |

The bulk toggle appears automatically on the refund page when an order has more than a few items. When active, all selected items share the same refund settings (restock, fee handling, and cancellation fee).

<Note>
Cancellation fees require the **Cancellation fees** feature to be enabled, and the staff member must have the **Apply cancellation fees** permission.
</Note>

### Refunding to Account Balance

Instead of returning money to the original payment method, you can credit the refund amount to the customer's account balance. This is useful when you want to retain the revenue while giving the customer credit for future purchases.

Account balance refunds:
- Require the **Account balances** feature to be enabled
- Are not available for box office-only orders (orders placed without a customer account)
- Are not available for complimentary (zero-value) orders
- Can be configured to include or exclude processing fees and reseller fees

### Fee Handling During Refunds

When processing a refund, fees are handled based on your configuration:

- **Processing fees** — By default, processing fees paid by the customer are included in the refund. If the **Reseller fees are non-refundable** limitation is active, platform fees are deducted from the refund amount.
- **Reseller fees** — Depending on your configuration, these may or may not be included in the refund amount.
- **Refund protection fees** — If the customer purchased refund protection, these fees may be handled separately depending on the refund protection provider.

Refunds can also be processed programmatically. Use the API to [refund an entire order](/api-reference/orders/refund-order) or [refund specific items](/api-reference/orders/refund-order-items).

## Cancelling Items Without Refund

To cancel items without returning money:

1. Open the order in admin and click **Refund/cancel items**.
2. Select the items to cancel.
3. Choose **Cancel items** as the refund type.
4. Optionally choose whether to notify the customer.
5. Submit.

Cancelled items are removed from the order, stock is restocked, and the order status updates accordingly. No payment gateway transaction is created — the financial record simply reflects that the items were cancelled.

<Note>
Wallet top-up items that are cancelled will have the corresponding balance deducted from the customer's wallet. If the customer has already spent more than the wallet contains, the cancellation is blocked.
</Note>

### Cancelling Resale Tickets

<Tip>Resale ticket cancellation added in Release 48</Tip>

Tickets a customer bought through [primary resale](/user-manual/resale/introduction) can't be refunded, because the buyer's payment has already been paid out to the seller. They can be cancelled, though — for example to void a ticket after a chargeback on the original purchase, or after a resale that broke the event's terms. Cancelling moves no money: the buyer isn't refunded and the seller keeps their [payout](/user-manual/resale/selling-and-buying#how-sellers-get-paid).

On the refund page, resale tickets carry a **Resale** badge and a banner explains: *"Refunds aren't supported for resale tickets yet. Cancelling voids the ticket without returning any money to the customer."*

1. Open the buyer's order in admin and click **Refund/cancel items**.
2. Select the resale tickets and choose **Cancel items** as the refund type.
3. Decide whether to restock the tickets (see below).
4. Optionally choose whether to notify the customer.
5. Submit.

| Restock | What happens to the ticket's place |
|---|---|
| **On** | It is released back to general sale on the primary market. It does not return to the resale marketplace, and the seller's [listing](/user-manual/resale/selling-and-buying#listing-lifecycle-and-statuses) stays closed. The refund page confirms: *"Resale items will be released back onto the primary market and the sellers' original resale listings will remain closed."* |
| **Off** | It is held back from sale — stock and capacity limits are reduced so the place isn't sold again. |

<Note>
A resale ticket can only be cancelled once the resale has settled — the buyer's order is complete and the seller's payout has been processed. Until then (for example, while the buyer's order is still awaiting an invoice or payment link payment), cancelling is blocked with **We're currently unable to refund resale order items.** Any refund type other than **Cancel items** is blocked with the same message.
</Note>

Resale tickets can also be cancelled in bulk — see [Bulk Refunds](#bulk-refunds).

## Customer Self-Service Refunds

Customers can request refunds directly from their account if you enable the self-service refund feature. Self-service refunds are always processed as **account balance** credits — they are not returned to the original payment method.

### Configuring Self-Service Refunds

Self-service refund settings are managed through the account balance refund configuration:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Enable self-service refunds** | Allows customers to request refunds from their **My Account > Orders** page |
| **Cutoff hours** | How many hours before the event starts that self-service refunds are no longer available |
| **Refund processing fees** | Whether processing fees are included in the balance credit |
| **Refund reseller fees** | Whether reseller fees are included in the balance credit |

### How Customers Request a Refund

1. The customer goes to **My Account > Orders** and opens the order.
2. They click **Refund your order**.
3. They select which items to refund and provide a refund reason.
4. They confirm the refund.

The refund is credited to their account balance immediately.

### Self-Service Refund Restrictions

| Restriction | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Tickets only** | Only ticket items can be refunded through self-service. Products, fees, and other item types are not eligible. |
| **Before cutoff** | The event must be more than the configured cutoff hours away. Items beyond the cutoff show an error. |
| **Bundles** | If refunding a bundle, the lead item must be included. Bundles containing non-ticket items cannot be refunded via self-service. |
| **Discounted orders** | If the order contains discounted items, all non-bundled items must be refunded together (partial refunds are not available on discounted orders). |

<Note>
Customers who need a refund to their original payment method (rather than account balance) should contact the organiser. The self-service option always refunds to account balance.
</Note>

## Bulk Refunds

For situations where you need to refund or cancel many orders at once — such as a cancelled or postponed event — use the bulk refund page. Open it from the event dashboard by clicking **Issue refunds**. It also opens automatically, with the event(s) pre-selected, when you [cancel an event from the event listing](/user-manual/event-management/event-cancellations#from-the-event-listing).

### Processing a Bulk Refund

1. Open the event dashboard and click **Issue refunds**.
2. The page lists the orders containing items from the event, grouped under the event. Tick the event to select every order that can be selected, or expand it to pick orders individually — expanding an order shows its items for the event. Only items linked to the event are refunded; an order that also contains items from other events is partially refunded. See [Choosing Which Orders to Include](#choosing-which-orders-to-include) for what each order's badges mean and which orders can be selected.
3. Optionally, filter the order list to narrow which orders are included:

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Payment method** | Only show orders paid with a specific payment method |
| **Sales channels** | Only show orders from a specific channel (online, box office, import, API, invoice, kiosk) |
| **Cancelled orders** | **Show** (the default) lists every order. **Show only** lists orders whose items for the event have all been cancelled — a refund also cancels items, so refunded orders are included. **Hide** lists only orders that still have live items for the event. See [Cancelling Now, Refunding Later](#cancelling-now-refunding-later). <Tip>New in Release 48</Tip> |

4. Configure the refund settings:
   - **Refund type** — **Refund online** (return the money to the original payment method via the gateway), **Refund to balance** (credit the customer's account balance — only offered when **Enable balance refunds in admin** is turned on in the [balance settings](/user-manual/account-balances/self-service-refunds#enabling-self-service-refunds)), **Refund offline** (cancel the items and record the refund without any gateway processing — you return the money yourself), or **Cancel items** (cancel the items without returning any money)
   - **Refund processing fees for the selected orders** and **Refund [platform name] fees for the selected orders** — Whether processing fees and platform fees are included in the refund. Not shown for **Cancel items**.
   - **Restock items for the selected orders** — Whether to release the items' capacity back to sale. When unticked, the page warns that stock and capacity limits will be reduced instead.
   - **Cancellation fee** — An optional fee deducted from each refund, distributed proportionally across items based on their refundable amounts. Not shown for **Cancel items**. Requires the [cancellation fees](#cancellation-fees) feature and the **Apply cancellation fees** permission.
   - **Refund reason** — Refund reason for records
   - **Send a notification to all of the purchasers of the selected orders** — Email each customer about their refund or cancellation, with optional **Additional email text** to include in the message
5. Click **Refund** and confirm.

<Note>
The bulk refund page requires the **Refund** permission for orders, and processing a **Cancel items** run also requires the **Cancel** permission. See the [permissions reference](/user-manual/system-access/permissions-reference).
</Note>

Each order is processed individually. If any individual order fails to refund (e.g. due to a gateway error), it is skipped and the rest continue, so one problem order never blocks the rest of the batch. After all orders are processed, you receive a **Bulk refund summary email** listing the orders that were refunded (with the amount and payment gateway) or cancelled, and any that failed.

After you submit, the platform takes you straight to the [Bulk Refund Status](#following-a-bulk-refund-in-real-time) page for the batch, where you can watch progress without waiting for the email.

<Warning>
Bulk refunds cannot be undone. Review the list of affected orders carefully before submitting.
</Warning>

<Note>
Orders that are already part of an in-progress bulk refund are held back from the picker until that batch finishes. If you open the Bulk refund page while a batch is still running, a banner reading **You have bulk refunds in progress** lists each in-flight batch with a **View status** link, alongside the note *"Orders included in these batches are excluded from the list below until they finish processing."* This prevents an order being included in two batches at once.
</Note>

### Choosing Which Orders to Include

<Tip>Order status and refund protection badges added in Release 48</Tip>

Each order in the list shows badges so you can see where it stands before you select it:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cancelled** | All of the order's items for the event have been cancelled, but no money has been paid back yet — a refund is still owed. |
| **Refunded** | All of the order's items for the event have been cancelled and money has been paid back. The order is settled. |
| **Protected** | The customer bought refund protection for the order. |
| **Claimed** | A refund protection claim has been paid out on the order. |

The **Protected** and **Claimed** badges only appear when [Protect Group](/user-manual/refund-protection/protect-group) or [manual refund protection](/user-manual/refund-protection/alternative-providers) is enabled. Orders containing resale tickets are also labelled **Resale**, and orders created through an [order modification](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications) are labelled **Modified**.

Which orders you can select depends on the refund type. Orders that can't be selected are shown struck through, and switching the refund type automatically deselects any orders the new type can't act on.

| Refund type | Orders you can select |
|---|---|
| **Cancel items** | Orders that still have live items for the event. Orders whose items are all cancelled can't be cancelled again. |
| **Refund online**, **Refund to balance**, **Refund offline** | Orders with items that still have money to return — including items that were cancelled without a refund. Orders with nothing left to return can't be selected: those already refunded, those whose tickets were replaced through resale or an order modification, and those with a refund protection claim paid out (**Claimed**), since the customer has already been reimbursed. |

<Note>
An item that has had any money paid back counts as refunded here, even if retained fees or a cancellation fee leave part of its balance behind. To return that remainder, open the order and refund it individually — see [Refunding Cancelled or Partially Refunded Items](#refunding-cancelled-or-partially-refunded-items).
</Note>

The page loads up to 500 orders at a time. Orders that still have live tickets for the event are listed first, then cancelled orders still awaiting a refund, then settled orders — newest first within each group — so each run you complete sinks to the bottom and the next batch is ready at the top. When the list reaches the limit, the page warns you that the refund needs to be completed in multiple steps.

<Note>
Some orders can be selected but fail when processed, with the reason shown on the [Bulk Refund Status](#following-a-bulk-refund-in-real-time) page:

- Orders containing resale tickets can only be processed with **Cancel items** — any other refund type fails with **We're currently unable to refund resale order items.** When your selection includes resale orders, the page reminds you that cancelling voids the tickets without returning any money. With **Cancel items** and restocking selected, their places are released back onto the primary market. See [Cancelling Resale Tickets](#cancelling-resale-tickets).
- Tickets carried over through an order modification can't be refunded or cancelled in bulk — the order fails with **We're currently unable to refund modified order items.** Process these orders individually from the order page.
</Note>

### Cancelling Now, Refunding Later

<Tip>Cancel-then-refund support added in Release 48</Tip>

When an event is cancelled or postponed, you may want to void every ticket straight away but issue — or decide on — the refunds later. The bulk refund page supports this as two separate runs, using the **Cancelled orders** filter to separate the orders that still need each step:

1. **Cancel the tickets.** Set **Cancelled orders** to **Hide**, choose **Cancel items**, select the orders, and submit. The tickets are cancelled, so they're no longer valid, and no money moves.
2. **Refund them later.** When you're ready, return to the page, set **Cancelled orders** to **Show only**, choose **Refund online**, **Refund to balance**, or **Refund offline**, select the orders, and submit. The refund is issued against the items you cancelled in the first run, and their badge changes from **Cancelled** to **Refunded**.

The filter's help text summarises this: *"Orders cancelled without a refund can still be refunded here. Hide them to cancel the remaining orders, or show only them to issue their refunds."*

<Note>
Stock is only adjusted when items are first cancelled, so decide on **Restock items for the selected orders** during the cancellation run. When you later refund items that were already cancelled, their stock isn't adjusted a second time.
</Note>

### Following a Bulk Refund in Real Time

<Tip>Real-time status page added in Release 47</Tip>

Every bulk refund has its own **Bulk Refund Status** page, reached automatically after you submit or from the **View status** link on the Bulk refund page. The page refreshes itself while the batch is running, so you can leave it open and watch orders resolve. You can also return to it later using the same link — the URL is tied to the batch.

At the top of the page you see:

- A status banner: *"This bulk refund is still processing. The list will update automatically as orders are resolved."* while in progress, then either *"All orders in this batch were refunded successfully."* or a message naming how many orders could not be refunded.
- **Batch ID** (copyable, useful when contacting support), **Initiated by**, **Initiated at**, and **Last updated at**.
- A **Progress** line with a count badge for each state: **Queued**, **Pending**, **Succeeded**, and **Failed**. A progress bar fills as orders resolve.

Below that, the **Orders** table lists every order in the batch with its current **Status**, the **Refund** amount, the **Payment gateway**, when it **Resolved at**, and a **Details** column. Each order moves through these states:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Queued** | Waiting to be processed. The details column shows *"Awaiting processing"*. |
| **Pending** | Submitted to the payment gateway, which has not yet confirmed the outcome. The details column shows *"Awaiting payment gateway confirmation"*. |
| **Success** | The order was refunded. |
| **Failed** | The order could not be refunded. The reason is shown in the details column. |

The page stops refreshing once the batch is complete (no orders left **Queued** or **Pending**).

### Processing a Pending Refund Manually

A refund that the gateway has accepted but not yet confirmed sits in a **Pending** state until the gateway's confirmation webhook arrives (see [Tracking In-Flight Refunds](#tracking-in-flight-refunds)). In almost all cases the webhook arrives on its own and you should simply wait.

Occasionally a webhook never arrives — for example in a mis-configured environment — leaving the order stuck on **Pending** indefinitely. For these cases you can resolve the pending refund by hand from the order's payment history:

1. Open the order and find the pending refund transaction in its payment history.
2. On that transaction, open the **Update status** menu (shown only for a refund still in the **Pending** state).
3. Choose **Mark as successful** or **Mark as failed**, then confirm.

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Mark as successful** | Runs the same follow-up as a real successful refund: the refunded items are cancelled (and restocked if restock was selected when the refund was created), the customer is emailed a confirmation if notifications were enabled, the order's refunded total and status update, and any linked integrations (such as accounting or refund protection) run. |
| **Mark as failed** | Marks the refund as failed in the order's transaction history. The items stay active — nothing is cancelled or restocked — and you can attempt the refund again. |

<Warning>
Only update a pending refund's status once you have confirmed the actual outcome directly with your payment provider. Marking a refund successful does not move any money — it only records the outcome and runs the follow-up actions. If a pending refund was part of a bulk refund, resolving it here also advances that order on the [Bulk Refund Status](#following-a-bulk-refund-in-real-time) page.
</Warning>

### Tracking In-Flight Refunds

Some payment gateways — including Revolut and Nuvei, as well as certain Stripe card types — return a **pending** status when a refund is submitted. The actual outcome arrives later via a webhook from the gateway. Bulk refunds now track these delayed refunds end-to-end so you have full visibility on what's still in progress and what's been confirmed.

| Email | When it sends | What it contains |
|-------|---------------|------------------|
| **Bulk refund summary** | Immediately after the bulk refund finishes queueing | A list of orders that refunded instantly (success or failure) and a separate **pending** section for orders awaiting confirmation from the payment gateway. |
| **Bulk refund update** | Once the last pending refund in the batch resolves | A final breakdown showing which pending orders ultimately succeeded and which failed, with gateway transaction IDs to support reconciliation. |

The summary email also notes: *"Some payment gateways do not provide instant feedback. You will receive a follow-up email once they have all been confirmed by the payment provider."*

#### Why You'd See a Pending Section

Pending entries are normal for any bulk refund involving an asynchronous gateway. While a refund is pending, the platform won't allow another refund on the same order — protecting against duplicate refunds. If you try to include an order with an in-flight bulk refund in another bulk refund, the system blocks it with the message: *"The following orders already have a refund in progress and cannot be included."*

The follow-up **bulk refund update** email is the source of truth for the final outcome of pending refunds. Until you receive it, treat any orders in the pending section as still in progress — even if the customer reports something different.

## Refund Eligibility

Not all items or orders can be refunded. The following conditions prevent a refund:

| Condition | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Order is frozen** | The order is locked during an active [modification](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications) or resale listing. Cancel the modification or wait for it to complete first. Once a modification completes, refund the replacement order — see [Refunding a Modified Order](/user-manual/order-management/order-modifications#refunding-a-modified-order). |
| **Item already refunded** | The item has no refundable balance left. A cancelled or partially refunded item that still has a remaining balance can be refunded — see [Refunding Cancelled or Partially Refunded Items](#refunding-cancelled-or-partially-refunded-items). |
| **Item replaced by resale or modification** | The item's value was already returned elsewhere (resale proceeds or a balance transfer to the replacement order). Refund the replacement order instead. |
| **Resale ticket** | Tickets bought through resale can't be refunded: **We're currently unable to refund resale order items.** Once the resale has settled they can be cancelled without a refund instead — see [Cancelling Resale Tickets](#cancelling-resale-tickets). |
| **Refund protection claimed** | The customer has already been reimbursed through their refund protection policy: **This item has already been refunded via the refund protection policy and cannot be refunded again** |
| **Nothing to pay back** | The retained fees and any cancellation fee would cover the whole balance of the selected items: **This refund would pay nothing back, because the retained fees and any cancellation fee cover the full balance of the items selected.** |
| **Claimed gift voucher** | Gift vouchers that have been redeemed by the recipient cannot be refunded. |
| **Pending refund in progress** | Another refund is currently being processed for this order (common with asynchronous payment gateways). Wait for it to complete. |
| **Deposit item** | Deposit payments are non-refundable through the standard refund flow. |
| **Third-party sync pending** | The order is awaiting a response from a third-party integration. The refund is blocked until the sync completes. |

### Async Gateway Refunds

Some payment gateways (such as Adyen) process refunds asynchronously. When you submit a refund through these gateways:

1. The refund transaction is created with a **Pending** status.
2. You see a confirmation that the refund has been submitted.
3. The gateway confirms the refund later (typically within minutes to hours).
4. Once confirmed, the refund transaction updates to **Success**.

While a refund is pending, additional refunds on the same order are blocked.

## Order Status After Refund

The order status updates automatically based on the outcome:

| Scenario | Resulting status |
|---|---|
| All items refunded (money returned) | **Refunded** |
| Some items refunded, others remain | **Partially refunded** |
| All items cancelled (no money returned) | **Cancelled** |

## Email Notifications

The system can send emails at each stage:

| Action | Recipient | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Refund processed** | Customer | Confirmation with refund amount and affected items |
| **Cancellation processed** | Customer | Notification that items have been cancelled |
| **Bulk refund summary** | Admin user who initiated | Initial breakdown of confirmed refunds plus any pending refunds awaiting gateway confirmation |
| **Bulk refund update** | Admin user who initiated | Follow-up sent once all pending refunds in a batch resolve, showing the final outcome of each |

Customer notifications are optional — you can choose whether to send them when processing each refund.
