## Overview

Holds are reusable access groups for seating restrictions.

You can apply holds to seats (and optionally general admission spaces, depending on feature context) so only permitted users can sell those spaces in operational tools such as box office flows.

## Access And Feature Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Seating plan holds feature** | Holds management is available only when this feature is enabled for the company. |
| **Permissions** | You need hold permissions to view/create/edit holds. |
| **Seating plans prerequisite** | Holds are an extension of seating plans and depend on seating-plan availability. |

An additional permission, **Sell from any seating plan hold**, controls broader hold-based selling access.

## Creating A Hold

From **Seating plans → Holds**:

1. Click **Add new hold**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Set a **Colour**.
4. Optionally choose a **Hold category** (see [Hold Categories](#hold-categories)).
5. Assign **Hold users** (admin users for the current company).
6. Submit.

Validation includes unique hold naming and user validity checks.

## Hold Contexts

Holds support two feature contexts:

| Context | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Seats only** | Holds can restrict seat-based spaces. |
| **Seats + general admissions** | Holds can also be applied to general admission spaces (with hold-level stock controls at event level). |

## Managing Existing Holds

The holds index shows operational metadata for each hold:
- Number of associated users
- Number of events using the hold
- Number of seating plans where the hold is applied

You can:
- Edit hold details
- Soft delete and restore holds
- Filter seating plans by hold usage from the seating plans list

## Hold Categories

<Tip>Hold categories added in Release 47</Tip>

Hold categories group related holds so you can audit their capacity together. The admin describes them as *"a grouping mechanism when auditing hold capacities on aggregate within the Event Audit report."* For example, you might put every sponsor hold in a **Sponsors** category and every production hold in a **Production** category, then check how many seats each group is holding across an event.

In the [Event audit report](/user-manual/reporting/default-reports), each category adds two columns under a **Hold categories** heading — for a **Sponsors** category, **Sponsors capacity** (the stock held by holds in that category) and **Sponsors remaining** (how much of that held stock is still available).

### Managing Hold Categories

Navigate to **Events > Seating plans** and open the **Hold categories** tab. The tab is also on the **Holds** page, and appears when seating plan holds are enabled for your company and you have permission to view hold categories.

- **Create a category** — Click **Add new hold category**, enter a **Name**, and click **Submit**. Names must be unique within your company.
- **Rename a category** — Click the category in the list, change its **Name**, and click **Submit**. Holds keep their category when it's renamed.
- **Delete a category** — Click its delete icon. A category can't be deleted while a hold is assigned to it — you'll see *"This hold category cannot be deleted whilst it is in use."* Remove the category from those holds first. Deleted holds still count, so a category that was assigned to a hold before it was deleted can't be deleted either.

### Assigning a Category to a Hold

Each hold can belong to one category. Set it with the **Hold category** field, which appears:

- When you create or edit a hold from the **Holds** tab.
- On an event's [plan dashboard](/user-manual/event-management/seated-events#plan-dashboard), when you restrict spaces to a hold — for a new hold created with **Add new hold**, or for an existing hold you have permission to edit.

Start typing to choose an existing category. If you have permission to create hold categories, you can also type a new name — the category is created when the hold is saved. Without that permission, you can only choose from existing categories. To take a hold out of its category, clear the field.

<Note>
A hold and its category must belong to the same company. If you work across a parent company and its child companies, a hold can only use categories created by the company that owns the hold — otherwise saving shows *"The selected hold category must belong to the same company as the hold."*
</Note>

Hold categories have their own **Hold categories** group in the role builder, with the standard create, view, edit, and delete actions. See the [Permissions Reference](/user-manual/system-access/permissions-reference#other-event-related-groups).

## Holds on the Event Dashboard

When an event uses a [seating plan](/user-manual/seating-plans/introduction) with holds applied, hold data is displayed directly on the [event dashboard](/user-manual/event-management/introduction). Each hold shows its name, ID, and capacity information — making it easy to check hold usage at a glance without navigating to the seating plan.
